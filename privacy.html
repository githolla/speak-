Privacy

Personal without becoming communal.

Shared hardware doesn't mean shared data. The physical environment becomes part of the privacy model.

Ask: “What did I talk about with ChatGPT earlier?” Speak knows whether that conversation belongs in the room.

For sensitive information: “I can answer that privately on your phone.”

Whisper.

You can talk quietly. Speak can answer quietly. At night:

“What time is it?” “2:14.”

No glowing screen. No waking everyone up.

Private mode.

Touch and hold. The microphones physically disconnect. The light changes. Everyone in the room can see that Speak isn't listening.

Privacy shouldn't require trusting an icon buried in settings. It should be visible.

Talk privately.

“Send this to my phone.”

A conversation can seamlessly move from the room to a personal device. Sensitive subjects don't need to be spoken aloud just because the interaction began with Speak.

You decide what Speak remembers, what it forgets, and what remains private.
