Thinking

Brainstorm out loud.

“I'm trying to figure something out.” Speak can become a thinking partner — challenge an idea, organize thoughts, remember unfinished ideas, connect something you said earlier.

Your spoken thoughts become usable knowledge instead of disappearing.

Wait. That's related to the thing you mentioned Tuesday about customer onboarding.

Capture ideas instantly.

“I just had an idea.” Say it. That's enough. Speak can save it, categorize it, connect it to a project, expand it later, or remind you when it's relevant.

“Show me the ideas I've had about the new product.”
Your shower thoughts finally have a database.

Meetings without meeting notes.

Put Speak in a room with permission from everyone participating. It can capture decisions rather than simply producing transcripts. Afterward:

“We made three decisions, assigned four actions, and left two questions unresolved.” “Why did we decide against option B?”

Speak can retrieve the reasoning behind the decision.

Work without sitting at your computer.

“What's happening with the Anderson project?” “Anything I need to respond to?” “Give me the important stuff from Slack.” “Draft a response to Kelly.” “Read it.” “Make that less formal.” “Send it.”

The computer becomes available while you're walking, cooking, getting dressed, or staring at a whiteboard.
