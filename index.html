The idea

A humanlike companion that lives in the home.

Powered by ChatGPT and speaking through GPT-Live, Speak controls your smart home accessories, answers questions, plays media, and responds to messages — in realtime, natural conversation.

And it learns more about you over time, becoming more personalized and more proactive the longer it lives with you. Less like operating a device. More like knowing someone.

Philosophy

Ask less.

The evolution of computing has mostly required humans to learn how computers want to be used. Speak reverses that relationship.

You shouldn't have to know which application controls the thermostat. Which calendar contains the appointment. Which store sold the product. Where the receipt lives. Which streaming service has the movie. Which person sent the message.

You tell Speak what you're trying to accomplish. It figures out the rest.
