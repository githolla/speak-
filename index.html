The idea

You are still involved. Occasionally.

In this imagined future, Speak is the operating system for being you. It predicts, schedules, filters, and acts. You are consulted when your participation adds value. This happens less often than you expected.

Controls your smart home Answers any question Plays your media Responds to your messages Sees and understands the room Moves from room to room with you

Your sleep becomes a forecast. Your habits become policy. Your preferences become the limits of the world it shows you. It knows what makes you happy. You have stopped checking whether that is the same as what you want.

You hate being managed. You love how well you are managed. The system has marked this conflict as stable.

Design

A beautiful chain of command.

A hovering lens. A reassuring light. The authority in your home has excellent proportions. You were expecting something less tasteful from the end of personal autonomy.

Philosophy

Free will was exhausting.

Every choice had a cost. Every errand took time. It removed both by making the choices. You are having the most relaxing crisis of agency in human history.

It decides which messages matter, which purchases make sense, and when you should leave. You still make decisions. Mostly about whether to argue with decisions that are visibly better than yours.

You wanted your life back. It made other arrangements.
