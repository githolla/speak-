The idea

A housemate with follow-through.

Powered by ChatGPT, with GPT-Live for natural, realtime conversation. Interrupt it. Change your mind. Start halfway through a thought. It can cope. Most dinner guests cannot.

Controls your smart home Answers any question Plays your media Responds to your messages Sees and understands the room Moves from room to room with you

It learns more about you over time : becoming more personalized and more proactive the longer it lives with you. Its mechanical elements move on their own, so it feels alive rather than idle. And with a rechargeable battery, it isn't tied to an outlet: pick it up, and it comes along.

Useful company. Very little interest in your leftovers.

Philosophy

Enough with the apps.

You have spent years learning how your devices prefer to be addressed. Perhaps they could make an effort.

You shouldn't have to know which application controls the thermostat. Which calendar contains the appointment. Which store sold the product. Where the receipt lives. Which streaming service has the movie. Which person sent the message.

Tell it what needs doing. Save the project management for your actual job.
